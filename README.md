# TradeQuery

**Learn SQL by running a trading company.**

TradeQuery is a planned web-based intelligent tutoring system that combines introductory SQL exercises with a business simulation. Players explore company data, make decisions and receive guidance as they learn.

![Concept art showing the company office and SQL exercise interface](course%20documentation/gamePic.png)

## The idea

Players manage a small company that buys products from suppliers, stores them in a warehouse and fulfils customer orders. They write SQL queries to find the information needed to make decisions: which products are running low, which supplier offers fit the budget or which employee can handle a warehouse task.

The system is intended for learners with little or no SQL experience. Its first two learning objectives are:

- **Filtering data** with `SELECT` and `WHERE`.
- **Combining related data** from multiple tables with `JOIN`.

## Planned learning experience

The initial scenario will guide players through a short sequence of business tasks. For example, they might find products stored in the wrong warehouse zone and then identify an employee who can move them.

The tutor is planned to check query results, provide hints in stages and track attempts and hint usage for each skill. This information can help identify topics where a learner needs more practice.
